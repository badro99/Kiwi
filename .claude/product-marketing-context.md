# Kiwi · product marketing context

Shared context for every marketing, copy, ad and video task on Kiwi. Read it before
asking the owner questions. Last updated 2026-11-08. The product facts below were
checked against `main` on 2026-10-30; the partner ships fast, so re-check any feature
against `origin/main` before it becomes the punchline of a piece.

---

## 1. Product overview

- **One line:** Kiwi is the till and back office for Moroccan restaurants, cafés and shops. One device, one caisse, the owner's whole business on their phone.
- **Landing line:** « Restauration et commerce de détail. Deux métiers, la même caisse. »
- **What it does:** the merchant sells at the till (iPad, tablet, phone or computer, with thermal printers, cash drawer and scanners). Every sale, table, stock movement, client and shift lands on the owner's dashboard live. It works offline and in French, English and Arabic.
- **Category (how people look for it):** caisse enregistreuse, logiciel de caisse, système de caisse pour restaurant / café / boutique, POS Maroc.
- **Type:** B2B SaaS, monthly subscription, tiers from Basic to Ultimate (multi-store is Ultra). Some modules are paid options (OrderPro).
- **Brand umbrella:** Kiwi Systems. Kiwi Pay, Banking and Investing are future ideas: never market them.
- **Founders (public):** Badr-Eddin Bakkioui (CEO) and Zakariae Attahiri (CTO, COO), Tanger.

**Never put a price, a discount figure, an investment ask or a revenue projection in any external content.** Describe tiers by name only. Moroccan business culture handles numbers face to face. Public macro data (tourist arrivals, SME count, interchange cap) is fine.

## 2. Target audience

- **Who:** independent Moroccan merchants, 1 to ~5 locations, owner-run.
  - Deepest fit: restaurants, cafés, café-brunch, snack, pasta/fast-casual; clothing boutiques (incl. caftan, abaya, djellaba); maison/home stores.
  - Also live: pressing, épicerie (carnet de crédit). Thinner tills (pharmacie, librairie, fleuriste, spa, coiffure, gym): verify before featuring.
- **Decision maker:** the owner (often also behind the counter). Sometimes a manager or the owner's son/daughter who is more at ease with tech and pushes the switch.
- **Users:** cashier, waiter, cook, stock keeper. Low patience for screens, high turnover.
- **Jobs to be done:**
  1. Know what I made today and where the money is, without being in the shop.
  2. Stop losing money to mistakes, theft, forgotten tabs and stock I can't see.
  3. Run the service fast (tables, kitchen, deliveries) with staff who change often.

## 3. Personas

| Persona | Role | Cares about | Kiwi promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patron de café / resto | Decision maker + user | Cash at the end of the day, staff honesty, service speed at rush | Every table and every dirham visible on his phone, live |
| Gérante de boutique | Decision maker | Stock by size and colour, Instagram/WhatsApp orders, deliveries and returns | Sizes and barcodes that make sense, Ozon parcels from the till, money counted when it really arrives |
| Le fils / la fille du patron | Champion | Modernising the family shop without a fight | Easy for the staff, and the parent sees the numbers |
| Le comptable | Influencer | Clean monthly figures, invoices with ICE | A secret accountant link and a monthly dossier |

## 4. Problems and pain points (open content here)

Open on a physical object or a real scene, never on a screen:
- The cahier (notebook) with crossed-out sums; the end-of-day count that never matches.
- The call to the livreur: « fin wsel l colis ? », COD money that arrives weeks later, or not at all.
- The dusty jacket on the hanger: stock nobody knows is there; the size that "should" be in stock.
- The confused tourist reading a French-only menu.
- The kitchen shouting, a ticket lost, a table forgotten.
- The owner who cannot leave the shop because nobody else knows the numbers.
- Clients who came every week and stopped, and nobody noticed.

**What it costs:** money that leaks quietly, time, and the owner's freedom to be away.
**Emotional tension:** distrust (of staff, of livreurs), fatigue, "I'll do it tonight", fear of tech that breaks during service.

## 5. Competitive landscape

> Owner to confirm and complete with named local competitors.

- **Direct:** other POS / caisse software sold in Morocco, often a cash register plus a separate back office.
- **Secondary:** a separate app for each job (menu app, stock app, delivery app, Excel) that don't talk to each other.
- **Indirect / the real competitor:** the cahier, the calculator, WhatsApp and memory.

When comparing with paper or Excel, say plainly that any good till does the basics (« n'importe quelle bonne caisse le fait »). Never claim "first", "only Kiwi" or "new" without checking.

## 6. Differentiation (build beats on these)

Distinctive and live:
- **AI menu scan:** a photo or PDF of the menu becomes the carte in the till.
- **« Scanner ma salle »:** a photo or sketch of the room becomes a floor plan.
- **Menu in 23 languages** for tourists; nutrition and allergens.
- **Arabic kitchen screen**, and a kitchen tablet with no printer needed.
- **Morning briefing:** the owner wakes up to what changed (sales drop, low stock, cash gap…).
- **Client book with WhatsApp drafts in French or Darija.** Kiwi writes, the owner sends by hand.
- **Accountant secret link.**
- **Ozon Express inside the till:** an Instagram/WhatsApp order typed once becomes a parcel, tracking, label, and the money counted when it is really paid (store must link its Ozon account).
- **Boutique sizes and readable barcodes:** one barcode per size, colour and size readable in the code, automatic product photos chosen by name.
- **Claude connector:** the owner can plug Kiwi into Claude (read-only by default).
- One device, every trade, real Arabic (RTL), works offline.

**Basics are background, never the punchline:** kitchen ticket, staff codes, manager code, Z report, sales on the phone, price changes reaching the till, store credit, loyalty points, ordering from the table. Every till has them.

## 7. Objections and anti-personas

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| « Ma caisse marche déjà » / « J'ai mon cahier » | Keep the habit, lose the leak: show the one thing the cahier can't (morning briefing, stock by size, the client who stopped coming). |
| « Mes employés ne savent pas utiliser ça » | Personal codes, roles per trade, a till built for rush hour; on-site training and guides are included. |
| « Et si internet coupe ? » | Kiwi keeps selling offline and sends everything when the connection is back. |
| « C'est cher » | Don't argue numbers in content. Talk about the money already leaking. Pricing is for the conversation. |

**Anti-persona:** large chains needing deep ERP integration out of the box; merchants who want food-delivery platform integration (not offered).

## 8. Switching dynamics

- **Push:** end-of-day counts that don't match; can't leave the shop; lost COD money; stock surprises.
- **Pull:** seeing the shop live from anywhere; tools that feel made for Morocco (Darija drafts, Arabic kitchen, Ozon, caftan photos).
- **Habit:** the cahier works "well enough"; the old caisse is paid for; staff know the old way.
- **Anxiety:** breaking service on day one; staff resistance; data loss; a foreign-feeling tool.

## 9. Customer language

**Speak like the merchant, in plain, precise Darija for social video** (French for written B2B, Arabic where it fits):
- Words to use: flouss, sel3a, colis, livreur, l'caisse, l'cahier, kaysalek, jib, « ch7al wsel l jibek ».
- Name the money and the object, not the feature.
- If a Kiwi label is French (« Stars », « Bon d'avoir »), say what it means in Darija.
- Short punchline endings with rhythm, e.g. « Sold machi b l7ess. Sold b l7ssab. »

**Words to avoid:** dashboard, KPI, analytics, insights, AI-powered, seamless, revolutionary, game-changer, all-in-one platform, leverage, unlock, empower, "the future of retail".

**Glossary:** caisse / till · Z de caisse (end-of-day report) · bon (kitchen ticket) · plan de salle (floor plan) · carte (menu) · bon d'avoir (store credit) · réassort (restock) · établissement (one store) · Order Pro (QR/NFC ordering add-on).

> Owner to add 3-5 real merchant quotes when available.

## 10. Brand voice

- **Personality:** warm, precise, calm, confident, local.
- **Tone:** direct and concrete, like a good friend who runs a shop. Never cocky, never techy.
- **Do:** show, not tell; one idea per piece; Kiwi appears as the answer in one or two beats, shown working; end on a punchline.
- **Don't:** list features; brag about basics; invent numbers; use US startup vocabulary; claim anything in the never-claim list.

## 11. Style guide

- No em dashes (U+2014) in user-facing copy: use commas, full stops or middle dots (·).
- No italics anywhere. Emphasis by weight or colour.
- No emojis in titles or CTAs.
- Type: Inter Tight for Latin, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic. No bold display weights.
- Colours: Atlas #0B6E4F, Riad #053B2C, Mint #7DF2B0 (accent, under 5% of the surface), Paper #F7F5F0 (never pure white backgrounds), Ink #0A0F0D. No new accent colours. The secondary Bougainvillée palette only when explicitly asked.
- Icons: Google Material Symbols only.
- Product names: Kiwi, Kiwi Systems, Order Pro, Kiwi AI. Plans: Basic, Plus, Pro, Ultra, Ultimate (no prices).
- Use fictional café/shop data in visuals; never real merchant figures or staff names.

## 12. Proof points

- Logos on the landing: MIX Restaurant, Pasta Corner, Claro, Startoner.
- Real merchants in active use include Santos (boutique, Ozon) and Pasta Corner.
- Amira Café / Boutique / Pressing is the owner's **test account**, never present it as a customer.
- No metrics, testimonials or figures to cite yet. **Do not invent any.** Owner to supply real quotes.

## 13. Content and channel context

- **Main channels:** Instagram Reels and TikTok (9:16), WhatsApp, Facebook; LinkedIn for partners and investors.
- **Video house style:** Inter Tight write-on type synced to the voice, the payoff word on the mint pill, brand-white backgrounds (not dark green), real Kiwi screens rebuilt as clean vector UI, official logo with text only under it. Each new film needs its own concept, transitions and music.
- **Sequencing:** awareness first. Early pieces build a following; a feature gets one beat of mention or tease, never a walkthrough. Deep dives wait for that topic's own series.
- **Curate:** bring 3-5 strong ideas with the reason each one hooks, not a menu of features.
- **SEO themes (French, then Arabic):** caisse restaurant Maroc, logiciel de caisse boutique, caisse enregistreuse café, gestion de stock boutique vêtements, menu multilingue restaurant.

## 14. Goals

- **Primary goal now:** awareness. Make Kiwi known to Moroccan restaurant, café and boutique owners.
- **Conversion action:** a WhatsApp message or a demo request, then an on-site visit.
