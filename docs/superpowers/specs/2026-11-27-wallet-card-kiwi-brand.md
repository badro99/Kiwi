# Kiwi loyalty card

The card is a small piece of the Kiwi product: a deep green face, warm paper and a quiet mint progress indicator. A soft field and fine texture give the web card depth, while the balance and reward remain easy to read.

- Web: riad and atlas face, paper text and QR plate, mint labels and progress; bone paper for the signup surface.
- Apple: riad background, paper foreground, mint labels and strip; supplied Kiwi wordmark and icon.
- Google: riad background, Kiwi icon and the same mint-on-riad strip.
- Dashboard and stand: the same Kiwi identity, with the shop name as content and the bilingual offer close to the QR.

The shop logo remains on the web card where there is space. It is omitted from the pass strip so progress stays clear at every size and the pass artwork remains consistent across merchants.
