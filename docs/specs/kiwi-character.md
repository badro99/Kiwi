# Kiwi official character · v1.0

The owner selected the plush kiwi-fruit character as Kiwi's official character on 2026-09-28. It complements the brand; it does not replace the Kiwi wordmark or favicon.

## Canonical design

- Rounded kiwi-fruit silhouette: cocoa-brown plush exterior, kiwi-green face and belly, one dark-green leaf sprout.
- Small glossy black eyes, rosy cheeks, minimal embroidered mouth, little arms and feet.
- Forest-green café apron with a cream round patch carrying the **exact** `assets/kiwi-favicon-new.svg` artwork. The mark must not be redrawn by an image model.
- Soft, curious and helpful; tactile 3D plush rather than a flat icon, a human character or a new corporate logo.

## Approved transparent assets

All six master PNGs are 1024 × 1536 with transparent alpha in `assets/mascot/`. The matching 600 × 900 WebP files are optimized for the web brand guide.

| Asset | Pose / expression | Intended moment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-neutral` | Gentle smile, raised hand | Default welcome and Kiwi IQ introduction |
| `02-thoughtful` | Hand at chin, eyes looking up | A question or insight in progress |
| `03-wave` | Happy eyes, waving | Greeting and onboarding |
| `04-aha` | Wide eyes, open mouth | Discovery or explanation |
| `05-listening` | Seated and attentive | Support and listening |
| `06-celebrate` | Arms up, delighted | Milestones and success |

## Placement

- Use the approved transparent artwork without mirroring, stretching, recoloring, or cropping the leaf and feet.
- Keep it separate from financial amounts, tappable controls, alerts, receipts and payment confirmation. It should never imply a real Kiwi IQ answer when the content is illustrative.
- In marketing and onboarding, let the character support the message rather than cover copy. At small sizes, prefer a larger crop or omit it if the apron favicon cannot be recognized.
- The wordmark remains the primary brand identifier. The favicon on the apron is a clothing detail.

## Source and provenance

The original base illustration and five poses were made with the built-in image-generation tool, using the base as an identity reference. The character images were generated with blank apron patches. The real repository favicon SVG was then composited onto each patch in HTML and rendered as transparent PNG, avoiding a lookalike mark. Original generated cutouts and composition templates are retained in the working character kit outside the repository; only approved, branded exports are committed here.

This character decision does not imply a trademark registration or a physical merchandise design approval. No social campaign was posted as part of the character work.
