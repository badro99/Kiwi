## Critical accuracy fixes vs. the current rendered section

The current `index.html` copy contains three claims that the research above should correct:

1. **Date label "1er nov. 2024" for the interchange cap is misleading.** Bank Al-Maghrib's decision is *Décision réglementaire n° 244/W/2024* dated **20 sept. 2024**, with the cap **effective 1er oct. 2024**. The "1er nov. 2024" date matches the *separate* CMI prohibition on signing new merchants (from the Conseil de la Concurrence decision), not the interchange cap. **Recommend**: split into two events, or relabel.

2. **"vs 1,20–1,30 % auparavant"** — the actual previous level documented by BAM is **1,20 %** flat. The "1,30 %" upper bound is not in the primary source. **Recommend**: change to "vs 1,20 % auparavant" (single value).

3. **"+12 autres établissements en discussion avancée"** in the pilots-section disclaimer (already removed in the marquee redesign) was unverified. Not a why-now claim, noted for completeness.

## Facts I could NOT fully verify (need human input or another pass)

- **Exact date of the 19,8 M tourist announcement.** The Ministry communiqué references "2025 résultats" but no single press date appears as 5 janv. 2026 specifically. The current site uses "5 janv. 2026" as the marker date; the closest confirmed date is the **publication of the bilan 2025** (early 2026, day not pinned). **Recommend**: change date label to "Début 2026 · bilan tourisme 2025" or pin to a verifiable communiqué date.
- **2017→2024 pre-cap interchange range.** Some industry articles reference 1,30 % as a ceiling, but BAM's own communiqué cites 1,20 %. The 1,30 % figure was likely a pre-2020 transitional level. Treat 1,20 % as the canonical pre-cap baseline.
- **Number of buyer EDPs at 31 janv. 2026 cession.** Le Desk lists six cessionnaires; medias24 lists five. The discrepancy is because one EDP (a sixth bank subsidiary) entered after the initial press round. **Recommend**: state "cinq à six cessionnaires" or "les principales filiales bancaires acquéreuses" rather than a fixed number.
- **Percentage of Moroccan retail still cash-only.** Often cited at 95 %+ but not verified in this Phase 1 search; the 1 % electronic-transactions figure from the Conseil de la Concurrence is the inverse and is the authoritative number to cite.
