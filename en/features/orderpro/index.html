OrderPro · Kiwi Pro

QR code ordering at the table: guests order, your till stays in charge.

OrderPro puts your menu on the guest’s phone. They scan the QR code on their table, pick their language, build their order and send it. The order reaches your till first, the team accepts it, then it goes to the kitchen. Payment stays with you, at the table or at the counter.

The essentials

  • One QR code per table. Guests scan and order from their phone, with no app to install.
  • The order reaches the till first. A team member accepts it, then it goes to the kitchen.
  • The price comes from your published menu, never from the phone.

01 · The menu

Your menu, in the guest’s language.

In Marrakech or Tangier, the same room can seat a family from Casablanca and tourists who do not read French. Each of them reads the menu in their own language.

  • The menu shows in French, English and Arabic, and in other languages if you publish their translations.
  • Options, extras and set menus: nothing is pre-selected, and a missing required choice is flagged before adding.
  • Nutrition values and the 14 allergens come from your complete recipes, and guests can filter.

02 · At the till

Nothing goes to the kitchen without your say.

A QR order that goes straight to the kitchen means a dish cooked for an empty table, or a teenager’s prank. OrderPro sends every order through the team.

  • The order reaches the till first. A team member accepts it, then it goes to the kitchen.
  • Once accepted, the kitchen ticket prints exactly once and the kitchen display receives the options.
  • The price comes from your published menu, never from the phone.
  • A double tap or a slow network does not create a duplicate order.
  • An accepted order can still be cancelled. An order already paid cannot.

03 · At the table

Guests follow their order and call when they want.

The waiter no longer has to walk by ten times to find out if the table wants the bill. The table says so itself.

  • Guests see where their order is, call a waiter or ask for the bill from the table.
  • At the till, the bill splits by item or into equal shares. On their phone, guests work out their share, and payment is taken at the till.
  • No online payment: guests pay at the table or at the counter, as usual.

04 · The service

The menu keeps up with the room.

The dish of the day has run out, the kitchen closes at 11 pm, someone wants takeaway. OrderPro handles all three.

  • A sold-out dish comes off the menu, until a set time if you want, and an order for it is refused.
  • Outside your published hours, or when the till is off, the menu stops taking orders.
  • A QR code or a link for takeaway, paid at the counter.
  • A public booking link, with time slots calculated from your tables and opening hours.

Frequently asked questions

Do guests have to download an app?

No. The QR code opens a web page in the phone’s browser. Guests pick their language and order.

Do orders go straight to the kitchen?

No. Every order reaches the till first. A team member accepts it, and only then does the ticket go to the kitchen.

Can guests pay from their phone?

No. OrderPro takes no online payment. Guests pay at the table or at the counter, and the bill can be split at the till, by item or into equal shares.

Which languages does the menu show in?

French, English and Arabic, and other languages if you publish the translation of your dishes.

How much does OrderPro cost?

OrderPro is included in Kiwi Pro, at 399 MAD a month, or 349 MAD a month on the annual plan. The first 15 days are free with no commitment, and Kiwi takes no commission on orders.
