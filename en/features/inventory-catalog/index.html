01 · Live stock

Stock moves when you sell.

Accurate stock starts at the till. If sales and stock live in two tools, they contradict each other within the first week.

In Kiwi, the till and the stock share the same data. The dashboard shows stock value, stock-outs and what needs attention.

  • Every sale takes the item out of stock. In a restaurant, it also takes out the recipe’s ingredients.
  • Set a threshold per item. Below it, Kiwi alerts you, and again if the item runs out.
  • Batches can carry an expiry date, and Kiwi spots the ones that have expired.

02 · Stock count

A count that explains the variance.

A variance is only useful if you know where it comes from: a loss, a breakage, a typing mistake. Kiwi keeps a record of every movement so you can trace it back.

  • At a stock count, you count, Kiwi shows the variances and the owner approves.
  • Every correction becomes a dated movement with its author, never a sale.
  • Losses and breakage are recorded with their exact reason.

03 · Boutique

One model, all its sizes and colours.

A caftan in four sizes and three colours fits on one record, with stock for each variant. Nobody wants to create twelve items.

  • From Kiwi Plus: one record per model, stock per size and per colour. Selling the black one leaves the white one untouched.
  • From Kiwi Plus: the purchase price of each item, and the margin that follows.
  • When goods arrive, Kiwi prepares as many barcode labels as pieces received.

04 · Suppliers

What comes in, and from whom.

Stock does not only move through sales. Goods arrive, sometimes go back to the supplier, and every delivery should leave a trace.

  • Suppliers, goods received and supplier returns are included. Purchase orders and invoice matching are reserved for Kiwi Ultra.
  • The catalogue imports from a CSV file, including a French-format Excel export.

05 · Restaurant

Recipes that draw down the ingredients.

In a restaurant you sell a tagine, but you stock chicken, onions and takeaway boxes. Kiwi links the two.

  • From Kiwi Pro: a recipe sheet per dish, with its food cost calculated from your ingredient prices.

Frequently asked questions

Does stock go down by itself when I sell?

Yes. Every sale charged at the till takes the item out of stock. In a restaurant, a sale also takes out the recipe’s ingredients, according to their yield.

How does a stock count work in Kiwi?

You count the items, Kiwi compares them with the expected stock and shows the variances. The owner approves the count, and every correction stays in the history with its author.

Can I manage sizes and colours?

Yes, from Kiwi Plus. A model has a single record and stock for each size and each colour.

Can I import my existing catalogue?

Yes, from a CSV file. A French-format Excel export, with its semicolons, is accepted.

How much does Kiwi inventory management cost?

Stock tracking is included in Kiwi Basic, at 150 MAD a month or 99 MAD a month on the annual plan. The size and colour catalogue is in Plus, at 300 MAD a month. Recipes and food cost are in Pro, at 399 MAD a month. The first 15 days are free.
