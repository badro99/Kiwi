01 · Offline

The network drops, the sale goes on.

In Morocco, a fibre cut or a saturated 4G line on a Saturday night happens. A till that waits for the server before it can charge loses you the queue.

Kiwi records every sale on the device first. When the connection comes back, sales travel up to your dashboard in order.

  • Without internet, the till keeps taking payments. Sales wait on the device and go out as soon as the network is back, with no duplicates.
  • The till opens in the browser: PC, Mac, Android tablet or iPad. You do not need to install anything to start selling.

02 · Scanning and receipts

Scanning and the receipt, without the wait.

At the till, every second shows in the queue. You beep, you charge, and the receipt should follow without anyone thinking about it.

  • A USB scanner or the phone camera reads barcodes, leading zeros included.
  • The receipt prints by itself at checkout when a printer is set up. A reprint is marked as a duplicate.
  • Arabic receipts print as real letters. If the device cannot draw them, Kiwi refuses to print question marks.

03 · Printers

Your thermal printer, connected the way you want.

You often keep the printer already sitting on the counter. Kiwi speaks ESC/POS, the language of most receipt printers, and uses whichever route works in your shop.

  • Over Bluetooth or USB, straight from Chrome or Edge, with no extra software.
  • With Kiwi Printer Bridge, on the counter computer (even an old Windows 7) or on an Android device, for network printers or printers already installed.
  • On an iPad, the till drops the receipt online and the counter bridge prints it. It pairs once, with a 6-digit code.
  • On the restaurant and grocery tills, the drawer opens when you take cash. A card payment does not open it.

04 · Cash float

A float at the start, a count at the end.

A till that does not balance means an awkward conversation at closing time. Kiwi replaces it with numbers everyone can see.

  • You open the till with a float and close it by counting. Kiwi shows the gap between expected and counted cash, and does not let a shortage be hidden.
  • Each employee has a personal code. Voiding a completed sale needs a manager’s code.
  • A discount needs a reason. One that falls outside your rules is flagged to the owner.

05 · End of day

The whole day, on one receipt.

In the evening you want to know how much came in, by which method, and what is still owed. Without redoing the sums by hand.

  • The daily report totals sales by payment method, net of refunds, and prints on the receipt printer.
  • Invoices carry the shop’s ICE, IF and RC numbers. The VAT on the invoice matches the receipt to the centime.

06 · Credit book

The credit book, without the notebook.

In a neighbourhood shop, you give credit to the neighbours. The notebook gets lost or stained, and nobody remembers the exact amount. Kiwi keeps each customer’s tab.

  • The credit book keeps what each customer owes, and every repayment, in full or in part.
  • From Kiwi Plus: returns, exchanges and credit notes, partial refunds included.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Kiwi till work without internet?

Yes. The till records every sale on the device, then sends it when the connection comes back. A sale replayed several times is counted once.

What hardware do I need to start?

A recent browser on a PC, Mac, tablet or iPad is enough to sell. To go further: an ESC/POS receipt printer, a cash drawer connected to the printer and a USB scanner. You can often keep the hardware you already have.

Can I print from an iPad?

Yes. Safari on iPad has no Bluetooth or USB access for the printer, so the till drops the receipt online and Kiwi Printer Bridge, installed on a computer or an Android device at the counter, prints it.

Can receipts be in Arabic?

Yes. Kiwi draws Arabic for the thermal printer, which avoids the question marks many printers show instead of the letters.

How much does the Kiwi till cost?

Kiwi Basic costs 150 MAD a month, or 99 MAD a month on the annual plan, which is 1,188 MAD paid once. The first 15 days are free, with no commitment. Kiwi takes no commission on your sales.
