Guide 01 · MenuRestaurant menu engineering: classify dishes by demand and contribution.

Food-cost percentage answers one control question, but it cannot tell you what a popular dish contributes in dirhams. This guide calculates unit contribution margin and sales mix, then places four Moroccan dishes into the classic Star, Plowhorse, Puzzle and Dog matrix.

The worked table uses 900 sales and reconciles to 47,600 MAD of total contribution. It also adds the operational veto the basic matrix misses: preparation time, kitchen bottlenecks, waste, delivery commission and attached sales.

Popularity shows what sells. Contribution shows what remains.

Guide 02 · Break-evenRestaurant break-even: convert monthly costs into daily covers.

The break-even guide separates fixed, variable and mixed costs, calculates the contribution margin ratio and verifies the result in reverse. A 100,000 MAD fixed-cost example at a 58% contribution ratio reaches 172,414 MAD of monthly sales.

The same result becomes 2,028 equivalent covers per month and 78 per day at an 85 MAD average check across 26 days. A browser-only calculator lets you change the four assumptions without sending or storing the values.

Choose the next decision

Start with the decision that blocks you now.

If prices and recipe costs exist but the menu still feels arbitrary, start with menu engineering. If the team is busy but the month still ends unclear, calculate break-even first. The two analyses connect: a stronger contribution mix lowers the sales needed to cover fixed costs.
