How we write these guides

Publish less, and make every page usable the same day.

  • Start from one precise operator decision, never from a keyword volume.
  • Show the formulas, the units, the assumptions and the limits of every example.
  • Never present a teaching figure as an average Moroccan price.
  • Cite a Moroccan primary source next to any tax or health claim, with the date it was verified.
  • Publish equivalent French, English and Arabic editions, with reciprocal hreflang.
Editorial promiseNo paid rankings, no invented data, and no universal target hidden inside an example. Every guide ends with an action, a grid or a calculation you can redo in your own venue.
